## 🗺️ What Is This?

Moving to a new country is overwhelming. This guide is written from **real first-hand experience** — not from a university brochure. It covers everything from the moment your plane lands at Brisbane Airport to navigating your first semester at QUT, with honest, practical, sometimes humorous advice that you won't find in any official student handbook.

> *"The guide nobody gave you — but every international student needs."*

## 📖 What's Covered

### ✈️ Arrival at Brisbane International Airport
Everything starts here. What to write on your **Incoming Passenger Card (IPC)**, why it matters more than you think, and what happens if you get it wrong. Includes links to official videos and real deportation case examples.

### 🌿 Biosecurity — Critical
Why you **cannot** bring your grandmother's spices, homegrown seeds, or any food from home. Australia takes biosecurity seriously — fines are hefty and customs officers are not negotiable.

### 📶 Communications — Getting Connected
- Free Wi-Fi at Brisbane Airport
- International eSIM options (AloSIM, Airalo)
- SIM cards from Telstra, Optus, Vodafone — what to watch for
- Why your 3G phone won't work in Australia
- Finding a custom/vanity number via Amaysim

### 🚌 Transport from the Airport
Every option ranked honestly, including:
- **Free**: QUT's 24-hour airport reception and transfer for new international students
- **Budget**: The **$1 Hack** — a step-by-step route from airport to the city using the free DFO shuttle + 590 bus
- **Convenient**: Uber, Didi, 13Cabs Maxi Taxi
- **Overpriced**: AirTrain (spoiler: it costs 45× more than a regular train)
- **50-cent fares**: Brisbane's permanent 50¢ TransLink public transport — buses, trains, and ferries

### 🏠 Australian Address — Why It's Everything
Your address is tied to every single thing — SIM cards, bank accounts, Medicare, government IDs. What to do if you don't have a permanent address yet, and why you should never use an Airbnb address for your bank card delivery.

### 🏦 Banking in Australia
- What documents you need to open an account
- Digital-only banks (ANZ Plus, UBank, UpBank) that skip the queue entirely
- The **Checking vs Savings ATM confusion** — why Australia's banking system feels like the movie Inception
- Why you **won't get a credit card** until you have a job
- Currency exchange tips at the airport (Prosegur Change, Travelex)
- The CommBank frozen account trap

### 🏢 Student Accommodation
Honest comparison of StudentOne, Scape, and UniLodge — all the way from the budget-friendly granny flat in Woodridge to the luxury 222 Brisbane Skytower. Includes a video link on apartment hunting.

### 😲 Culture Shock — Reality Check
Things that will surprise you about Australia:
- **220–240V power** — your 110V devices will fry without a converter
- **$40–60 for a pack of cigarettes** — yes, really
- **Barefoot culture** — people walk barefoot in shopping centres
- **No tipping expected** — service staff earn proper wages
- **Early closing times** — most shops shut by 5–6 PM
- **No bidets** — BYOB (bring your own bidet)
- **No bargaining** — prices are fixed (except car yards and markets)
- **Wildlife encounters** — possums, wallabies, and yes, snakes
- Budget tips: KMart, BigW, Costco, 7-Eleven coffee, 50¢ McDonald's ice cream

### 🎓 QUT Student Success Guide — Must Read
- The difference between a **Student** and a **"Student"** (and the visa consequences)
- Why LinkedIn matters more than Instagram
- **Canvas** — your one-stop portal for all lectures, assignments, and unit content
- **HiQ** — QUT's student support and volunteering hub
- How to make the most of QUT's 24/7 library
- Study habits, part-time work balance, and how not to get caught at the airport

## 🚀 Running Locally

No build step needed. Just open the HTML file in any browser:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/HashimsGitHub/IntlStudentGuide-QUT.git
cd IntlStudentGuide-QUT
open International-Student-Journey-at-QUT.html
```

Or on Windows:
```bash
start International-Student-Journey-at-QUT.html
```
